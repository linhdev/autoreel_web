About AutoReel

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Written by Bùi Quang Linh

AutoReel is an application that runs on your own machine — Windows or macOS — to create, process and publish videos in bulk. It is not a per-video subscription, not a cloud service that keeps your footage, and it does not require you to know how to edit.

This page exists so you can see who makes the software, which numbers it is built on, and what it deliberately does not do. If you are considering a trial, this is worth reading before the pricing table.

Why AutoReel exists

Selling with video repeats the same chain: pick a product, write the lines, record the voice, cut the footage, add subtitles, brand it, publish. Every step has its own tool, and most of the time goes into moving between those tools.

AutoReel puts that chain into one workflow. You choose a source — a product link, an existing video, or an idea — configure it once, and let the machine run the batch. What is left for you is picking the product and writing the opening line for the right viewer.

The numbers today

The three figures below were counted from the app's own database on 9 October 2026. They are not estimates and they are not rounded up:

  • 501 videos generated.
  • 189 workflows completed.
  • 2 platforms integrated for publishing: Shopee Video and Facebook/Reels.

These are the numbers of one person using the software for their own work, not of a marketing campaign. When they change, the home page changes from the same source.

How we work

  • Local-first. Video and data stay on your machine. The software does not keep your footage on a server.
  • No invented testimonials. No fabricated reviews and no customer logos that do not exist.
  • Limits are written down. If a source cannot be downloaded, a platform has no subtitles, or a feature only runs on Windows, the documentation says exactly that.
  • No per-video charge. You pay for the software, not for the number of times you press render.
