Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Applies to autoreelvn.com and the AutoReel software

This page states what the site and the video downloader collect, how long it is kept, and how you can ask for deletion. AutoReel runs on your own machine: video, images and project data are not sent to our servers.

When you only read the site

The site is static HTML. There are no accounts and no login cookie. Cloudflare handles HTTP requests and may keep technical logs (IP address, time, path, user-agent) under Cloudflare's own policy. We do not use that data to identify you.

Google AdSense may set advertising cookies on pages that show ads. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's ad settings. We do not control cookies set by Google.

When you use the video downloader

  • The video link is sent to the download queue. It is not shown publicly.
  • Your IP address is hashed with HMAC and a private key, only to count quota (3 videos/hour, 10/day). We do not store the raw IP.
  • Downloaded files are stored on Cloudflare R2 and deleted after about 2 hours; the job record is deleted after 7 days.
  • Push notifications (if you enable “Notify me when it finishes”): the browser issues a push endpoint; we keep it with the job id to send one notification, and remove it when the subscription expires.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks the submission to stop spam.

When you buy or activate the software

The licence server receives the activation code, device id and plan information to verify that the licence is active. We do not receive your video content, project files or AI API keys. API keys are stored on your machine and sent directly to the AI provider.

Who we share with

We use Cloudflare (hosting, queue, file storage), Google AdSense (advertising) and a payment provider when you buy. We do not sell personal data and do not share it with anyone beyond those providers.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email support@autoreelvn.com with enough information to identify the record (when you used the tool, a job id if you have one). We answer within 30 days.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the product news or sent to the email used for a purchase.
